Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 8, 2026

At Bharat Horizon, we prioritize the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information we collect, record, and how we utilize it.

1. Information We Collect

When you utilize our search inputs (e.g. searching flight segments or states), this data is processed locally to query files. When you perform affiliate redirects to Skyscanner or Booking.com, we transmit location parameters to set the affiliate partner trackers.

2. Cookies & Web Beacons

We use standard cookies to optimize your browsing preferences, store search history logs locally, and manage affiliate tracking parameters. These cookies do not harvest personal details.

3. Third-Party Links

Bharat Horizon contains multiple outbound links to affiliate flight booking systems and equipment checklists. We have no direct control over the privacy protocols of these third-party platforms.

If you have any questions regarding this policy, contact our support desk at support@bharathorizon.com.
