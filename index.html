🌤 Best Time to Visit

Oct – Mar: Ideal countrywide — festivals, sightseeing, cool weather.
Apr – Jun: Perfect for Himalayan escapes and hill stations.
Jul – Sep: Monsoon magic in Kerala, Goa, and Northeast India.

🛂 Travel Essentials

E-Visa: Apply online at least 10 days before arrival.
Connectivity: Buy a local 4G/5G SIM at any major airport.
Payments: UPI is universal; carry cash for rural areas.

🏥 Safety & Insurance

Medical Insurance: Always include evacuation cover.
Water: Drink bottled mineral water; skip ice at road stalls.
Emergency: Police 112 · Tourist Helpline 1363.
