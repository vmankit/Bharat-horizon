2 DAYS

Weekend City Break

Designed for quick getaways. Explore the city centers, major historical landmarks, and local dining hubs without rushing.

Perfect for:

Delhi, Chandigarh, Puducherry, Bangalore weekenders.

Schedule Highlights

Day 1: Central Heritage & Culture

Check into a heritage stay, take a guided tour of the primary fort or palace complex, and enjoy traditional dishes in the local bazaar.

Day 2: Nature Spots, Shopping & Departure

Visit botanical gardens, art galleries, and explore handicraft stores for souvenirs before catching an evening flight.

3 DAYS

Extended Explorer

Deep dive into local traditions. Perfect for exploring natural wonders like hill stations, desert sands, or beach sanctuaries.

Perfect for:

Goa beaches, Jaipur forts, Munnar tea gardens, Rishikesh rafting.

Schedule Highlights

Day 1: Historic Hub & Orientation

Walk through ancient markets, visit three central historical monuments, and taste regional street food.

Day 2: Wilderness / Scenic Day Hike

Take an excursion to the nearest waterfall, scenic lake viewpoint, or spice plantation. Watch a traditional evening dance.

Day 3: Handicrafts & Leisure Departure

Shop at artisan silk/clay cooperatives, enjoy a relaxed cafe lunch, and transfer to airport/station.

5 DAYS

Grand Tour Experience

Our most complete road trip guide. Covers historical capitals, national wildlife parks, rural communities, and wellness resorts.

Perfect for:

Kerala backwaters, Ladakh desert road trips, Uttarakhand temple tours.

Schedule Highlights

Day 1-2: State Capital Sights & Fine Dining

Tour royal palaces, museums, and historical quarters. Dine at award-winning restaurants.

Day 3: National Park Safari & Nature Trek

Wake up early for a guided tiger reserve safari or bird-watching hike. Rest at an eco-lodge.

Day 4: Rural Village Walk & Culinary Feast

Walk through crop farms, experience weaving/pottery, and eat an organic farm lunch.

Day 5: Souvenirs Shopping & Departure

Buy spices, handlooms, or silver items, pack bags, and take an aviation transfer home.
