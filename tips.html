Visa & Entry Requirements

Citizens of most countries are eligible to apply for an Indian Tourist E-Visa online. Make sure to submit your application on the official government portal at least 4 to 10 days before your departure. Keep digital and printed copies of your E-Visa authorization letter.

E-Visa Checklist:

  • ✓ Passport validity of at least 6 months with two blank pages.
  • ✓ Recent color photograph with a white background.
  • ✓ Copy of passport personal details page.
  • ✓ Return ticket or onward travel ticket booking details.

Navigating Around India

India features one of the world's largest railway networks and multiple low-cost domestic air carriers, making regional hops easy.

🚂 Indian Railways

Book tickets in advance via the IRCTC portal or verified agencies. Third AC (3AC) or Second AC (2AC) sleeper tiers are recommended for long overnight segments.

🚕 Mobile Ride-Hailing

Use Uber, Ola, or Rapido apps in metropolitan cities for fixed, secure, and hassle-free transit rates. Avoid unmetered auto-rickshaws.
